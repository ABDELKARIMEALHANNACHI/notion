# Notion — Program Policy

## Rules
- Detailed, reproducible reports required
- One vuln per report (or chain for impact)
- Duplicates: first report wins
- Multiple vulns from one root cause = one bounty
- No social engineering
- No privacy violations / data destruction / service degradation
- Only test accounts you own or have permission for
- Reports covering multiple assets: paid once at highest asset

## Test Plan
- Self sign-up at notion.so
- Use HackerOne username + email for test account
- Create fresh accounts per engagement
- Cross-workspace testing = only workspaces created for engagement

## Response targets
- First response: 3 business days
- Triage: 10 business days
- Resolution: varies

## Safe harbor
Authorized conduct, no legal action. Standard H1 safe harbor.

## Disclosure
- Do not discuss vulns outside the program without consent
- Follow H1 disclosure guidelines
- Escalation: H1 mediation only (NOT security@makenotion.com, NOT support)
